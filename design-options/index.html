About me

Liz Raphael Helgesen

With more than 40 years devoted to inner work, self-discovery, and spiritual growth, along with professional coaching experience since 2016, I help people create lives that reflect who they truly are and who they want to become.

My own journey has taught me the power of honesty, humility, self-examination, acceptance, and personal responsibility. These principles continue to shape how I live, how I connect with others, and how I support those seeking meaningful change.

I specialize in helping people move beyond limiting beliefs, unhealthy patterns, and behaviors that stand in the way of greater freedom, purpose, and connection.

My coaching is deeply personal, spiritually grounded, and rooted in compassion, connection, and the courage to change. I also incorporate Nonviolent Communication (NVC), the Language of Compassion developed by Marshall Rosenberg, to help foster healthier relationships, deeper intimacy, and greater understanding and empathy.

Every journey is unique. My role is not to tell you who to be, but to help you reconnect with yourself, recognize your own strengths, and discover the freedom that comes from living authentically.
